Why RideCamp

Electric transport that pays for itself

Riders who earn a living on two wheels need machines that start every morning and cost less per kilometre. We built around that reality: quick battery swaps, simple servicing and running costs that are easy to predict.

    Energy per kilometre is typically well below the cost of petrol for the same distance.
